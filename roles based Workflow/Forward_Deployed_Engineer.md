# Forward Deployed Engineer Workflow

## Role Description
The Forward Deployed Engineer is embedded within client organizations to provide technical expertise, accelerate product adoption, and bridge the gap between engineering teams and customer operations. This role focuses on hands-on implementation, troubleshooting, optimization, and driving customer success through direct technical engagement.

## Key Responsibilities
- On-site technical implementation and deployment
- Architecture design and system optimization
- Hands-on troubleshooting and issue resolution
- Customer training and knowledge transfer
- Performance monitoring and optimization
- Feedback collection and product improvement recommendations
- Relationship building and customer advocacy
- Documentation and best practices sharing

---

## Workflow Steps

### 1. Pre-Deployment Preparation

#### 1.1 Customer Onboarding & Discovery (1-2 weeks)
- Schedule kickoff meeting with customer stakeholders
- Understand customer's business objectives and technical environment
- Review existing infrastructure and systems
- Identify key technical contacts and decision makers
- Assess customer's technical maturity and skill levels
- **Deliverables**: Customer profile, technical environment assessment, deployment plan

#### 1.2 Pre-Deployment Planning (1-2 weeks)
- Create detailed deployment timeline and milestones
- Identify resource requirements and dependencies
- Develop risk mitigation strategies
- Plan communication cadence and escalation procedures
- Prepare deployment materials and documentation
- **Deliverables**: Deployment roadmap, resource plan, risk assessment

#### 1.3 Technical Preparation (1 week)
- Set up development and staging environments
- Prepare deployment scripts and automation tools
- Create rollback and disaster recovery procedures
- Conduct internal testing and validation
- Prepare troubleshooting guides and runbooks
- **Deliverables**: Deployment scripts, testing reports, runbooks

---

### 2. On-Site Deployment & Implementation

#### 2.1 Infrastructure Setup (2-4 weeks)
- Deploy infrastructure components (servers, networks, databases)
- Configure system settings and security parameters
- Integrate with existing customer systems and tools
- Set up monitoring and logging infrastructure
- Validate all components are functioning correctly
- **Deliverables**: Infrastructure documentation, configuration specs, validation reports

#### 2.2 Application Deployment (2-4 weeks)
- Deploy application to production environment
- Configure application settings for customer environment
- Perform data migration and validation
- Set up user accounts and access controls
- Conduct system testing and performance validation
- **Deliverables**: Deployment checklist, configuration documentation, test results

#### 2.3 Go-Live Support (1-2 weeks)
- Provide intensive on-site support during go-live
- Monitor system performance and user adoption
- Address critical issues immediately
- Conduct final user acceptance testing
- Celebrate successful launch with customer team
- **Deliverables**: Go-live status reports, performance metrics, issue logs

---

### 3. Technical Optimization & Performance Tuning

#### 3.1 Performance Analysis (Ongoing)
- Monitor system performance metrics and KPIs
- Identify performance bottlenecks and optimization opportunities
- Analyze resource utilization and capacity planning needs
- Benchmark performance against industry standards
- Document performance baselines and trends
- **Deliverables**: Performance reports, optimization recommendations, capacity plans

#### 3.2 System Optimization (Ongoing)
- Implement performance tuning recommendations
- Optimize database queries and indexing
- Fine-tune application configurations
- Implement caching and load balancing strategies
- Conduct regular system health checks
- **Deliverables**: Optimization logs, performance improvement reports, tuning documentation

#### 3.3 Scalability Planning (Ongoing)
- Assess current and future capacity requirements
- Design scalability architecture for growth
- Implement auto-scaling and load balancing
- Plan infrastructure upgrades and expansions
- Document scaling procedures and runbooks
- **Deliverables**: Scalability assessment, architecture diagrams, scaling procedures

---

### 4. Troubleshooting & Issue Resolution

#### 4.1 Reactive Issue Management (Ongoing)
- Respond to customer issues and escalations
- Diagnose root causes of problems
- Implement immediate fixes and workarounds
- Document issues and resolutions
- Communicate status updates to customer stakeholders
- **Deliverables**: Issue logs, resolution documentation, status reports

#### 4.2 Proactive Monitoring & Prevention (Ongoing)
- Set up comprehensive monitoring and alerting systems
- Conduct regular system health checks and audits
- Identify and address potential issues before they occur
- Implement preventive maintenance schedules
- Analyze trends to predict and prevent future issues
- **Deliverables**: Monitoring dashboards, health check reports, preventive maintenance plans

#### 4.3 Knowledge Transfer & Documentation (Ongoing)
- Create troubleshooting guides and runbooks
- Document common issues and solutions
- Train customer operations team on issue resolution
- Maintain updated documentation and procedures
- Build customer's internal troubleshooting capabilities
- **Deliverables**: Troubleshooting guides, runbooks, training materials

---

### 5. Customer Training & Knowledge Transfer

#### 5.1 Training Program Development (2-4 weeks)
- Assess customer's training needs and skill levels
- Develop role-based training curricula
- Create training materials (documentation, videos, guides)
- Set up training environments and sandbox systems
- Schedule training sessions for different user groups
- **Deliverables**: Training curriculum, training materials, training schedule

#### 5.2 Hands-On Training Delivery (2-4 weeks)
- Conduct role-based training sessions
- Provide hands-on demonstrations and walkthroughs
- Answer questions and address concerns
- Conduct practice exercises and simulations
- Assess training effectiveness and knowledge retention
- **Deliverables**: Training attendance records, assessment results, feedback summaries

#### 5.3 Super-User Development (Ongoing)
- Identify and train internal super-users and champions
- Empower super-users to support other team members
- Provide advanced training and certifications
- Create internal knowledge sharing programs
- Build sustainable customer support capability
- **Deliverables**: Super-user training materials, certification records, support procedures

---

### 6. Feedback Collection & Product Improvement

#### 6.1 Customer Feedback Systems (Ongoing)
- Conduct regular check-ins with customer stakeholders
- Gather feedback on product functionality and usability
- Identify feature requests and improvement opportunities
- Document customer pain points and challenges
- Prioritize feedback based on impact and frequency
- **Deliverables**: Feedback reports, feature request logs, prioritization matrices

#### 6.2 Product Feedback Loop (Ongoing)
- Communicate customer feedback to product team
- Advocate for customer needs and requirements
- Participate in product roadmap discussions
- Provide customer perspective on product decisions
- Track implementation of customer-requested features
- **Deliverables**: Feedback summaries, product roadmap input, implementation tracking

#### 6.3 Best Practices & Optimization Recommendations (Ongoing)
- Share industry best practices with customer
- Recommend optimization strategies based on experience
- Document lessons learned and case studies
- Facilitate knowledge sharing across customer teams
- Identify opportunities for process improvement
- **Deliverables**: Best practices guides, optimization recommendations, case studies

---

### 7. Relationship Building & Customer Advocacy

#### 7.1 Stakeholder Engagement (Ongoing)
- Build strong relationships with customer stakeholders
- Conduct regular business reviews and check-ins
- Present performance metrics and success stories
- Address customer concerns and escalations
- Advocate for customer needs within the company
- **Deliverables**: Business review presentations, stakeholder reports, engagement logs

#### 7.2 Customer Success Metrics (Ongoing)
- Track customer adoption rates and engagement
- Measure business impact and ROI
- Monitor customer satisfaction and NPS scores
- Identify at-risk customers and intervention opportunities
- Report on customer success metrics to leadership
- **Deliverables**: Success dashboards, NPS reports, ROI analysis

#### 7.3 Internal Advocacy & Case Studies (Ongoing)
- Develop customer success stories and case studies
- Share customer wins and achievements internally
- Represent customer perspective in internal discussions
- Advocate for customer needs in product and engineering decisions
- Build internal awareness of customer value and impact
- **Deliverables**: Case studies, success story library, internal presentations

### 8. Documentation & Knowledge Management

#### 8.1 Technical Documentation (Ongoing)
- Create and maintain system architecture documentation
- Document configuration and customization details
- Maintain runbooks and operational procedures
- Document integration points and dependencies
- Keep vendor contact and support information current
- **Deliverables**: Architecture diagrams, configuration docs, runbooks, integration specs

#### 8.2 Customer-Specific Documentation (Ongoing)
- Create customer-specific guides and procedures
- Document customer's unique configurations and customizations
- Maintain customer-specific troubleshooting guides
- Create quick reference guides for common tasks
- Ensure documentation is accessible and up-to-date
- **Deliverables**: Customer guides, quick reference cards, procedure manuals

#### 8.3 Knowledge Repository (Ongoing)
- Maintain centralized knowledge base for customer
- Document lessons learned and best practices
- Create video tutorials and walkthroughs
- Build searchable FAQ and troubleshooting resources
- Enable customer self-service and continuous learning
- **Deliverables**: Knowledge base articles, video library, FAQ resources

### 9. Security & Compliance Management

#### 9.1 Security Implementation (Ongoing)
- Implement security best practices and standards
- Configure security settings and access controls
- Set up encryption and data protection measures
- Conduct security assessments and audits
- Address security vulnerabilities and risks
- **Deliverables**: Security configuration docs, audit reports, vulnerability assessments

#### 9.2 Compliance & Regulatory Requirements (Ongoing)
- Ensure compliance with relevant regulations (GDPR, HIPAA, SOC 2, etc.)
- Conduct compliance audits and assessments
- Maintain compliance documentation and certifications
- Monitor regulatory changes and updates
- Implement compliance improvements and updates
- **Deliverables**: Compliance audit reports, certification docs, compliance procedures

#### 9.3 Data Protection & Privacy (Ongoing)
- Implement data protection and privacy measures
- Monitor data access and usage
- Manage user access controls and permissions
- Conduct regular security training with customer team
- Maintain data backup and disaster recovery procedures
- **Deliverables**: Data protection plans, access control logs, backup procedures

### 10. Continuous Improvement & Optimization

#### 10.1 Regular Health Checks (Monthly)
- Conduct comprehensive system health assessments
- Review performance metrics and KPIs
- Identify optimization opportunities
- Assess customer satisfaction and engagement
- Plan improvements and enhancements
- **Deliverables**: Health check reports, optimization plans, improvement recommendations

#### 10.2 Quarterly Business Reviews (Quarterly)
- Present performance metrics and achievements
- Review customer goals and objectives
- Discuss roadmap and future plans
- Address customer concerns and feedback
- Plan next quarter priorities and initiatives
- **Deliverables**: QBR presentations, performance reports, action plans

#### 10.3 Annual Strategy & Planning (Annually)
- Conduct comprehensive customer assessment
- Review year-over-year performance and impact
- Plan strategic initiatives for next year
- Identify expansion and growth opportunities
- Renew or expand customer engagement
- **Deliverables**: Annual strategy document, strategic recommendations, renewal proposals

## Success Metrics

- **Deployment Success**: On-time, on-budget deployments with zero critical issues
- **System Uptime**: 99.9%+ availability target
- **Customer Adoption**: % of users actively using the system
- **Customer Satisfaction**: NPS score ≥ 50, CSAT ≥ 90%
- **Issue Resolution**: Average resolution time < 4 hours for critical issues
- **Performance**: System performance meets or exceeds SLA targets
- **Knowledge Transfer**: Customer team can independently manage system
- **Customer Retention**: Renewal rate ≥ 95%
- **Expansion Revenue**: Upsell and cross-sell opportunities identified and pursued
- **Time to Value**: Customer achieves ROI within target timeframe

- **Technical Expertise**: Deep knowledge of product, infrastructure, and integrations
- **Problem-Solving**: Ability to diagnose and resolve complex technical issues
- **Communication**: Clear communication with both technical and non-technical stakeholders
- **Customer Focus**: Commitment to customer success and satisfaction
- **Adaptability**: Ability to work in diverse customer environments
- **Documentation**: Strong documentation and knowledge sharing skills
- **Project Management**: Ability to manage complex deployments and timelines
- **Leadership**: Ability to influence and guide customer teams
- **Continuous Learning**: Commitment to staying current with technology trends
