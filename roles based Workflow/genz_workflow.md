## how to actually communicate 🗣️
- **no formality, ever** — we're not writing a cover letter here
- **be direct** — get to the point before they open tiktok
- **validate the struggle** — empathy > everything
- **inclusive by default** — gender-neutral, diverse, no one gets left out
- **make them respond** — ask questions, create dialogue, don't just monologue
